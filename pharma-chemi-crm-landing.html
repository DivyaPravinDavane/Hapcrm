The Trading Bottleneck

Why Pharma Trading Gets Messy

In high-speed API & chemical trading, delays mean lost orders. Without a unified system, your team faces 4 critical friction points.

Inquiries Scattered

Requirements arrive randomly over WhatsApp, emails, agent phone calls, and scribbled papers. Inquiries get overlooked before they are even logged.

Manual Stock Checks

Sales reps comb through outdated Excel files or call warehouse managers just to see if a chemical or API is in stock or available from regular suppliers.

Slow Quotations

Calculating freight, taxes, and margins takes hours. Buyers move to faster traders while your team is still calculating prices.

Lost Follow-ups

Quotes sit in buyers' WhatsApp chats without follow-up, and pending orders fall through the cracks without structured task tracking.
