The Trading Bottleneck

Why Pharma Trading Gets Messy

Pharma chemicals and API inquiries move fast. When inquiries live in five different places, sales slip away.

Scattered Inquiries

Requirements land across WhatsApp, personal emails, agent notes, and phone calls. Leads get lost before anyone logs them.

Manual Stock Checks

Sales reps scramble through outdated spreadsheets or call the warehouse just to check if an API or chemical is available.

Slow Quotations

Calculating freight, supplier rates, and delivery margins takes hours. Buyers move to faster traders before your quote is sent.

Lost Follow-ups

Without centralized status tracking and daily reminders, quotes sit without follow-up and pending orders fall through the cracks.
